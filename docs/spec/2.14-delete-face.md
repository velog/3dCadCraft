# 2.14 Delete Face

## 1. Purpose

Deletes faces of a solid or a surface body. With **Heal** the neighbouring faces grow until they close
the gap again (a fillet, chamfer, hole, pocket or boss is taken off, as when an imported part is simplified);
without it the body opens and becomes a surface body. **Lump or Void** deletes a whole shell: a separate
piece of the body (lump), or a cavity, which fills with material again (void). Inventor: 3D Model › Modify ›
Delete Face.

## 2. Inputs

- **Faces**: faces of one body (a solid or a surface body). With Lump or Void, one face of each shell to delete;
  a void is hidden inside, so it is reached with Select Other.

## 4. Browser and editing behaviour

- The feature is `DeleteFace<N>` (format revision 34, `BodyOp::DeleteFace`), with the Delete Face icon; its
  tooltip says how many faces and whether it heals.
- Edit Feature (double-click, Properties) reopens the panel with the feature rolled back.
- Right-click on faces offers Delete Face with the faces filled in (rule 17).

## 5. Edge cases and errors

- No face: OK stays disabled (*select the faces to delete*).
- Faces of two bodies: refused (*the faces belong to different bodies*).
- A face that no longer exists after an upstream edit: the feature's health.
- Heal with a curved neighbour (the round next to a deleted face): refused, naming both faces.
- Lump or Void on a body of one shell: refused (*the body has one lump and no void*).
- Every refusal leaves the part unchanged.

## 6. Implementation

- Data: `part::body_op::DeleteFaceOp` (`select`, `faces: Vec<FaceRef>`, `heal`).
- Geometry: `kernel::exact::delete_face` — `delete_faces` (SolidCraft `delete_faces` with Heal,
  `remove_faces` without), `delete_shell` (`vendor/` `delete_face.rs`); `part/exact.rs::delete_face` finds the
  faces in the solids and surface bodies and replaces the body. Body operations return the surface bodies
  themselves (`build_features` does not put them back after a `BodyOp` step).
- Names: every face left keeps its name; Delete Face makes no faces.
- GUI: `gui/delete_face.rs`.
- Tests: `tests/delete_face.rs` (a fillet, a chamfer, a hole, a pocket and a boss healed back to the plate; a
  box opened, then a face of the surface deleted; a void filled; a lump deleted; refusals; save / load);
  `tests/compat/r34`.
