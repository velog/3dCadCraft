# 1.9 Profiles and regions

Status: ✅ done · 🟡 partial · ⬜ to do · ⛔ waiting for another feature.

## 1. Purpose

The closed areas of a sketch are the **profiles** that Extrude, Revolve and the other features sweep. A rectangle with two circles inside gives three regions: a plate with two holes and two discs. The user picks the regions with a click. In Inventor a whole loop can also be picked (*Select Other → loop*).

## 2. Rules

| Rule | Status |
|---|---|
| Curves in the Normal style take part; construction and centerline ones do not | ✅ |
| Lines, arcs and circles are split at every intersection and tangency point | ✅ |
| Splines are joined only at their ends | 🟡 (⬜ spline intersections) |
| Dangling edges (open ends) are cut off; they do not affect the regions | ✅ |
| Nested loops: an island in a hole is a separate region | ✅ |
| The outer loop is counter-clockwise, the holes clockwise (the kernel's convention) | ✅ |
| Picking a **loop** instead of a region (Select Other) | ⬜ |
| Open profiles (for Surface or Match Shape) | ⛔ (Booleans, surfaces) |

## 3. Display

| Behaviour | Status |
|---|---|
| In the sketch being edited the closed regions are lightly filled | ✅ |
| With Extrude the proposed regions are pale green, the selected ones saturated green | ✅ |
| The regions are cached and recomputed only when the sketch changes | ✅ |

## 4. Persistence of the selection

The feature remembers the region by **the curves of its outer loop**, with a point inside as a fallback. So the selection survives dimension changes. If the region disappears, the feature becomes sick. ✅

## 5. Implementation

`sketch/profile.rs` (`Sketch::regions`, `Region`, `ProfileLoop`, `interior_point`), `kernel/mesh.rs` (`triangulate_with_holes`), `part/extrude.rs` (`ProfileRef`).
