# 5.4a Surface workflow: selection, feedback, visibility (review 2026-10-29)

## 1. Purpose

A review of how surface work feels in the app after the owner's test (`Surface_test`: two spline surfaces
trimmed by a third, then Loft / Patch between them, Copy Object of one). It collects what Inventor 2027 does
(and, for selection, what Creo does better), what we had, and the order in which the gaps are closed. The
command options themselves stay in [5.4-surfaces.md](5.4-surfaces.md) and [2.13-copy-object.md](2.13-copy-object.md).

## 2. Findings from the owner's test

| # | Seen | Cause | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | a picked surface does not light up; one cannot tell whether anything was selected | the highlight was drawn just outside the face along its normal; on a surface whose normal points away from the eye it lay behind the surface | ✅ 2026-10-29: painted on both sides of surface faces |
| F2 | command panels (Copy Object, Stitch, Trim…) show no prehighlight and no picks in the view | the planned panels never fed the selection drawing | ✅ 2026-10-29: picks blue, the item a click would take green, faces / bodies with their outline |
| F3 | hiding and showing bodies and surfaces is awkward | only the browser row's menu had Visibility | ✅ 2026-10-29: *Visibility (Alt+V)* in the menu of bodies, surfaces and faces, Alt+V, a hidden body picked in the browser comes back with Alt+V, *Show All Bodies* in the view's menu |
| F4 | Copy Object could not copy the surface | the owner's build had only the panel; with the logic (revision 40) a face of a surface is copied (checked on `Surface_test`) | ✅ |
| F5 | Trim: picking changes nothing, no preview of what is cut | Trim stores the clicked point only; nothing shows the region it removes | ✅ T1 2026-10-29: the side OK removes drawn see-through red, cut exactly at the tool (under the cursor, then the picked one; Invert Selection swaps); checked in the app on two crossing walls |
| F6 | Loft between the two trimmed surfaces is impossible | Loft sections are sketch regions, sketch points or planar face loops; edges of a surface are not sections | 🟡 L1 2026-10-29: edge sections (revision 41), two bridged by a ruled surface (G0) — works on `Surface_test` (`tests/surface_tools.rs`), checked in the app (preview outline, OK); Tangent / Smooth ⬜ |
