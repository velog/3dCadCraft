# 5.4a Surface workflow: selection, feedback, visibility (review 2026-10-29)

## 1. Purpose

A review of how surface work feels in the app after the owner's test (`Surface_test`: two spline surfaces
trimmed by a third, then Loft / Patch between them, Copy Object of one). It collects what Inventor 2027 does
(and, for selection, what Creo does better), what we had, and the order in which the gaps are closed. The
command options themselves stay in [5.4-surfaces.md](5.4-surfaces.md) and [2.13-copy-object.md](2.13-copy-object.md).

## 3. Inventor 2027 behaviour (our words)

- **Trim**: *Cutting Tool* (a surface, a face, a sketch curve, a work plane), then *Remove*: the areas to remove are
  picked one by one and **shown highlighted before OK**; *Invert Selection* swaps removed and kept areas. One trim works
  on one surface body; a surface with no area picked stays as it is.
- **Boundary Patch**: *Boundary* takes a chain of tangent-continuous edges (or sketch curves) closing a region; each
  edge has a *Condition*: Contact (G0, default), Tangent (G1) or Smooth (G2) — G1 / G2 only on an edge with one
  adjacent face (a free surface edge); continuous edges share one condition; *Weight* for G1 / G2. Further loops make
  islands. The patch is previewed before OK.
- **Loft** (surface output): sections may be open curves, **a set of continuous model edges** or a face loop. Next to a
  surface the section gets Tangent (G1) or Smooth (G2) conditions with a weight, so a loft between the free edges of two
  surfaces blends into both. This is the Inventor way to "connect two surfaces".
- **Visibility**: Alt+V toggles the visibility of the selection; hidden items are selected again from the browser
  (in assemblies also *Select All Invisible Components*, Shift + right-click) and shown with Alt+V.

## 4. Selection ideas from Creo (adopted where they fit Inventor's panels)

- **Chains** of edges, chosen when an edge is picked (a right-click query cycles them, a tooltip names the chain):
  *One-by-One*, *Tangent* (the edges tangent to the anchor), *Surface Loop* (the loop round a face), *Boundary* (the
  outer free edges of a quilt), each also *From-To* (a range between two picked edges).
- **Surface sets**: single surfaces, *Seed and Boundary* (a seed face and everything up to the boundary faces),
  *Loop* (the faces next to a loop of an anchor), *Solid* / *Quilt* (all faces of a body).
- What we take: in every edge selector (Patch, Loft edge sections, Extend, Ruled, Stitch's free edges) a picked edge
  expands to its **tangent chain** by default, with *Select Other* (resting the cursor) cycling **edge → tangent chain
  → free boundary of the surface**; the chain is shown as one highlighted pick. Face selectors get **Seed and
  Boundary** later (Copy Object, Delete Face, Thicken).

## 5. Order of work

| Step | What | Spec |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | Trim: the region under the cursor and each removed region drawn in the view (red, see-through) before OK; Invert Selection redraws them; the cutting tool highlighted | 5.4 Trim rows |
| L1 | Loft: edge sections (a chain of model edges, open or closed) with surface output; Tangent / Smooth conditions to the adjacent surface | 5.2 Sections, Conditions |
| L2 | Boundary Patch: chains (tangent chain by one click), per-edge G0 / G1 / G2 + weight | 5.4 Patch rows |
| V1 | boundary edges of surface bodies drawn (amber body, dark edges) | 0-ui |
| V2 | a face is planar only when its geometry is (free-form faces with a placeholder plane are curved) | 0.15 menus |
| S1 | edge chain selection (tangent chain, boundary of a quilt, Select Other cycling), shared by every edge selector | 0.9a |
| S2 | Seed and Boundary face sets | 0.9a |
