# 5.4a Surface workflow: selection, feedback, visibility (review 2026-10-29)

## 1. Purpose

A review of how surface work feels in the app after the owner's test (`Surface_test`: two spline surfaces
trimmed by a third, then Loft / Patch between them, Copy Object of one). It collects what Inventor 2027 does
(and, for selection, what Creo does better), what we had, and the order in which the gaps are closed. The
command options themselves stay in [5.4-surfaces.md](5.4-surfaces.md) and [2.13-copy-object.md](2.13-copy-object.md).

## 3. Plan of 2026-11-19 (branch `surfaces-2`)

The probe `tests/surface_goals.rs` (`cargo test --no-default-features --test surface_goals -- --include-ignored --nocapture
--test-threads=1`) builds the three products step by step; its report of that day: the bottle's loft and a drop embossed on
it hold, the engraved logo, the base fillet and every shell of an oval loft fail; the bumper's Freeform skin and three
trimmed openings hold, its Thicken fails; the hair dryer's barrel and handle hold, the junction fillet and the shell fail.
Order, by what blocks most of the products:

1. F20 shell of oval lofts (bottle, hair dryer) — the walls, not the offset, are the cause;
2. F21 surface loft through ovals (the bottle's surface route: window, patch, stitch, thicken);
3. F23 plastic features on the shelled products (Lip on the parting rim, Boss, Grill), the probe steps added;
4. F22 Thicken of trimmed Freeform skins (bumper), then the junction fillet and the engraved logo.
