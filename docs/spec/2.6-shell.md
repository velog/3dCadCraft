# 2.6 Shell

## 1. Purpose

Shell hollows a body so that only walls of a given thickness remain. The selected faces
(Remove Faces) disappear and become openings: a box without a lid, a housing, a cup. With no
face selected the result is a closed inner cavity. Shell acts on the whole body as it is at
the point of the feature. Features after it are not hollowed.

## 2. Inputs

- **Remove Faces:** faces to remove (optional).
- **Solids:** which body. Meaningful only in a part with several bodies.
- **Thickness:** wall thickness, the same for all (expression or parameter).
- **Unique Face Thickness** (optional): its own thickness for individual faces.

## 4. Browser and editing behaviour

- In the browser it is `Shell1`, after the features it hollows.
- Edit Feature opens the same panel with all values. The model is shown as it was before
  the Shell (rollback), and a failed edit keeps the old definition.
- The inner faces are named `Shell1:Inner[<the face they line>]`, e.g.
  `Shell1:Inner[Box1:Front]`. The outer ones keep their names. The remainder of the removed
  face (the rim around the opening) keeps the face's name.

## 5. Edge cases and errors

- Thickness ≤ 0: "Shell: the thickness must be greater than 0".
- The walls are thicker than half the body, or a round smaller than the thickness at the
  opening: the kernel refuses. The feature becomes "sick" with the reason (`the geometry kernel
  cannot build it (…)`), and the part stays as it was. It never falls back to the faceted
  kernel.
- A round at the removed face greater than or equal to the thickness (a box with all edges
  rounded, open at the top). The SolidCraft kernel refuses the rim ("the rim would have no
  thickness", #342), but Inventor builds it. We build the closed cavity, and the opening is
  cut as a prism along the outline of the face. The rim around the opening is a strip
  perpendicular to the face, and the walls have thickness t everywhere. The prism is wider by
  0.001·t so that it crosses the rounds instead of touching them (the kernel does not cut
  touching faces). For now only for an opening with straight edges. A circular opening (a disc
  with a rounded lid) is still refused.
- A round smaller than the thickness is allowed and gives a sharp corner inside (#319).
- Known limitations (tests in `tests/freecad_cases.rs`):
  - walls almost as thick as the round (r − t ≈ 0.01 mm at r 1): cutting the opening
    meets the inner rounds at a very small angle and the kernel refuses;
  - hemisphere: the kernel fails ("wire is not simple"), and the shared path offsets the
    sphere approximately (volume +0.36 %);
  - walls as thick as the part (cylinder 10 high, Shell 10 or 12): refused with "the walls are
    as thick as the part" (FreeCAD #25050).
- Checked cases from the SolidCraft issues: Shell after an extrusion with a −5° taper (#2178),
  a box with a cylinder along the edge (#1494). Both build for us.
- The removed face no longer exists (an edit before the Shell): "the face … no longer exists".
- Faces from different bodies: "the removed faces belong to different solids".
- A part built with the faceted kernel (an earlier feature with an option the exact kernel
  does not cover yet): "Shell needs the exact geometry kernel …".
- Mirror/Pattern of an individual feature does not copy the Shell (in Inventor neither).
  Copying the whole body works.
- After a Shell (Inside) the outer vertical edges are split in two at the level of the inner
  floor (a leftover of the Boolean cut). Fillet/Chamfer join them into one edge, and the chain
  on selection catches both pieces (tests "shell 2 then fillet").

## 6. Implementation

- `kernel::exact::shell_with(solid, open, thickness, ShellSide, rounded, prefix)`
  (`shell` = Sharp): Rounded Corners rounds the edges via `round_offset_corners`
  (convex/concave by probing the material around the edge, without the edges along the removed
  faces; new faces `<id>:Corner:Round:<edge>`), and
  `kernel::exact::faces_named`. Inside with openings goes through `sk::shell`. Outside, Both and
  the closed cavity use `sk::offset_faces` (the walls outwards) minus a cavity (the walls
  inwards, the openings pushed out by 2t + 1), like the SolidCraft engine.
- `part::shell::ShellFeature` (`Feature::Shell`, format revision 3), `FaceRef`,
  `ShellDirection` (Center, saved as `Both`), `ShellCorners` (revision 9); `part::exact::shell` finds the faces and the body; `PartDocument::add_shell`.
- GUI: `gui/shell.rs` (`ShellDraft`), the dialog and Properties in `gui/panels.rs` (`shell_dialog`, `shell_properties`).
- Tests:
  - kernel: 7 cases in `kernel::exact::tests::shell_*`;
  - part: `tests/matrix.rs`, the "shell …" cases, including Fillet and Chamfer before
    and after the Shell and an opening cut after the Shell;
  - Rounded Corners and Center: `tests/shell_cases.rs` (Outside ×3 sizes, Inside with an
    L-prism, box, Center, the name in the file); speed in `tests/perf.rs`.
