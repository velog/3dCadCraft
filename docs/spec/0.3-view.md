# 0.3 View: Orbit, ViewCube, axis triad

Section 0.3 in [FEATURES.md](../FEATURES.md).
Functional reference: Autodesk Inventor 2027. The text is our own; command and option
names stay in English.

## 1. Purpose

The tools the user views the model with: rotating around the model (**Orbit**), the
orientation cube in the corner (**ViewCube**) with the **Home** button and the compass,
and the small X/Y/Z axis indicator (**3D indicator**, ours is called the "triad"). They do
not change the model and do not go into Undo.

## 2. Inputs

- the mouse (drag, click, right click) on the view, the cube and the compass;
- keys F4 (Orbit), F6 (Home), Shift + middle button;
- the current camera: target, distance, `yaw`, `pitch`, `roll`, projection.

## 3. Options and behaviour

### 3.1 Orbit

| Option / behaviour | Inventor | Restrictions | Our status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free Orbit** (F4 + left, Shift + middle) | a horizontal drag rotates about the screen's vertical axis, a vertical one about the horizontal axis; the model can be rolled **over the top and the bottom** without stopping | — | 🟡 waiting for a visual check. We rotate about world Z (turntable) horizontally and over the pole vertically, with no limit on `pitch`. The view's "up" is d(eye)/d(pitch) = (−sin p·cos y, −sin p·sin y, cos p): continuous and defined also at ±90°. Upside down (cos p < 0), the horizontal drag flips its sign to follow the mouse. A rotated image (roll) first maps the drag back into the unrotated frame |
| **Constrained Orbit** | a horizontal drag rotates about the ViewCube's TOP–BOTTOM axis (like a turntable) | a separate command in the Navigation Bar | 🟡 the horizontal part of our Orbit is exactly this; there is no separate command |
| Circle and centre of rotation (Free Orbit) | inside the circle — 3D rotation, outside — about the view axis; the centre is chosen with a click | — | ⬜ we rotate about the camera target |
| Orbit during another command | works without interrupting the command | — | ✅ (navigation is independent of the commands) |

## 4. Browser and editing behaviour

None. The view is not part of the document and does not go into Undo; the file format
does not change. The "Home" view (Set Current View as Home) lives only in the session.

## 5. Edge cases

- Exactly over the pole (looking straight down/up) "up" is well defined: Top has FRONT
  at the bottom, Bottom has FRONT at the top.
- After an Orbit over the pole the camera is upside down; the picking rays (`ray`,
  `project`) stay correct, the cube and the triad turn over with it.
- Look At on an arbitrary plane is now exactly face-on (before, tilted planes with
  |n.z| > 0.9 were seen at 89°) and the plane's y axis points up; `roll` is used when
  needed.
- A transition interrupted by another camera change (Look At on a new sketch, Fit, Orbit)
  yields: the next frame does not overwrite the camera.
- A drag that started on the cube does not reach the model (no selection, no box, no
  manipulators), even if the mouse leaves the cube.
- The ring, seen from below, is drawn over the cube; picking always gives the cube
  priority.

## 6. Implementation

- `src/gui/camera.rs`: `OrbitCamera::up`, `upside_down`, `frame`, `set_frame` (yaw/pitch/roll
  from a direction + "up", chooses the branch with the smaller roll), `orbit` without a limit
  and with the sign flip, `turn_about_z`, `look_at` through `set_frame`, `is_ortho` by the
  view direction, `wrap_angle`; `ViewAnim` (time interpolation, `VIEW_ANIM_SECONDS`).
- `src/gui/viewcube.rs`: `pick`/`draw` (faces, edges, corners, arrows, roll, Home, compass),
  `ViewCube` (transition + dragging the cube/compass: `tick`, `animate`, `input`, `drag_hit`),
  `draw_triad`, `triad_origin`, `compass_direction`.
- `src/gui/mod.rs`: small hooks — `tick`/`input` at the start of `viewport`, `animate` in
  `set_view` and `cube_clicked`, `draw_triad` + `draw` at the end; navigation stays silent
  while the cube holds the drag.
