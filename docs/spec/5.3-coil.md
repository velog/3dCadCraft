# 5.3 Coil

## 1. Purpose

Coil winds a closed sketch profile around an axis along a helix: springs, helical grooves,
threads made "by hand". The profile is the cross-section of the coil; the axis is the line it
turns around and climbs along. With Cut, the coil cuts a thread into a cylinder.

## 2. Inputs

- **Profiles:** one or more closed regions of one sketch (the cross-section).
- **Axis:** a line of the same sketch (a centerline is picked automatically), an origin axis
  (X/Y/Z) or a work axis. It may point in any direction but must not intersect the profile.
- **Solids:** the bodies the operation works on (when there are several bodies).

## 4. Browser and editing behaviour

- In the browser it is `Coil1` (tooltip "Coil of Sketch1"); the consumed sketch sits under it and is hidden.
  Edit Feature opens the same panel; Properties shows the method, pitch, revolutions and height.
- The fields accept expressions and parameters: `pitch`, `revolutions`, `height`, `taper`,
  `start_transition`, `start_flat`, `end_transition`, `end_flat` (shown according to the method and
  the ends).

## 5. Edge cases and errors

- The axis intersects the profile: "the axis must not intersect the profile".
- The climb per revolution is smaller than the section: "the coil runs into itself …".
- Zero revolutions, pitch or height: "… must be greater than 0".
- The ends take more than all the revolutions: "the closed ends take more than all the revolutions".
- The taper reaches the axis: "the taper brings the coil to its axis".
- A Cut that does not intersect the body: "Cut: the coil does not intersect the body".
- A refusal leaves the part unchanged (test `coil_refusals_leave_the_part_unchanged`).

## 6. Implementation

- **Data:** `part::coil::CoilFeature` (`Feature::Coil`, format revision 14), `CoilAxis`,
  `CoilMethod`, `CoilEnd`; `PartDocument::add_coil`; `CoilFeature::sizes` gives the pitch, total
  angle and height; `CoilGeometry::at(a)` — height and radial offset at angle `a` (flat end,
  transition, constant pitch, transition, flat end).
- **Exact kernel** (`kernel::exact::coil`):
  - constant pitch without taper or ends — `sk::sweep_helix` (smooth B-spline walls);
  - a round section with ends or taper — `sk::sweep_path` along the path of the centre (smooth for
    any number of revolutions; the ends come out slightly rotated relative to the meridian plane);
  - any other section — a smooth loft through stations every 15°–45° (`sk::loft`);
  - if nothing works (many revolutions, the kernel makes linear walls) — a faceted body of
    triangles (`sk::mesh_body`, 36 stations per revolution).
  The attempt to assemble pieces (helix + loft) with a union failed: the kernel does not join
  touching B-spline bodies, and it took 83 s.
- **Booleans** (`kernel::exact::coil_boolean`): like the modeled thread (2.9) — the body
  is split into an exact part outside the ring around the coil and the material inside the ring; only
  the material in the ring meets the triangle mesh of the coil (`sk::faceted_boolean`). For
  Cut/Intersect the ring is in layers of ~4 mm, each on its own thread (6 revolutions in Ø20:
  12 s → 5 s). A Join that only touches the body (a spring on a plate) runs exactly.
  Unlike the thread, a Coil Cut is cut immediately (not after all features).
- **Names:** the walls are `<id>:Side:<segment>` (`<id>:Side` for a faceted body or in the zone),
  the first section `<id>:Start`, the last one `<id>:End`; a second profile — `<id>#2:…`.
- **Patterns:** records a `ToolSet` (the exact body and the operation), so Pattern/Mirror copy it
  (the copies are cut with the exact Boolean — slow for Cut).
- **Faceted path:** `CoilFeature::build_faceted` (polygons every 10°), for Surface and when
  the part is built faceted.
- **GUI:** `gui/coil_rib.rs` (`CoilDraft`, selectors Profiles → Axis, preview on a worker thread,
  OK/Apply/Cancel, Edit Feature, Properties), prompts EN/BG, F1 → this file.
- **Tests:** `tests/coil_rib.rs` (every method with the volume by Pappus ±1 %, left/right, taper, closed
  ends, Cut of a thread, Join to a plate, surface, the axis from a centerline, Flip, save/load,
  editing, refusals, SolidCraft #793 — half a coil cut by a plane, then a fillet on a
  shaft), `kernel::exact::coil::tests`, `tests/perf.rs` (three cases), fixture `r14/coil_rib_r14`.
