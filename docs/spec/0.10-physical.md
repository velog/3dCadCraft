# 0.10 Physical Properties

## 1. Purpose

Shows how much the part weighs and how its mass is distributed: mass, volume, area, centre
of gravity and moments of inertia. The mass comes from the volume and the material density
of each body (see [2.15-materials.md](2.15-materials.md)). The reference is Inventor
iProperties → Physical tab.

## 2. Inputs

- The part's bodies and their material (the body's, otherwise the part's, otherwise Generic).
- Accuracy (Requested Accuracy).
- Which bodies (Solids) in a multi-body part.

## 4. Editing behaviour

- The command computes the values as soon as it opens. After a change to the part, a
  material or the accuracy, the window shows "Out of date — Update" and the Update button
  is enabled (as in Inventor: disabled when the values are up to date). The key is a
  signature of the body, the features, the materials and the accuracy. The computation does
  not run every frame.
- Changing the appearance does not make the values stale (mass does not depend on colour).

## 5. Edge cases and errors

- Part without a body: "Physical Properties: the part has no solid body".
- Surface bodies are not counted (they have no volume).
- An open (non-watertight) body gives a wrong volume: such a body is skipped with a
  warning.
- Unknown material: the density of Generic + a warning.

## 6. Implementation

- `src/part/physical.rs`: `physical_properties(part, accuracy) -> Physical` — by the
  divergence (Gauss) theorem over the triangles of each body (tetrahedra from the origin:
  volume, first and second moments, exact for polyhedra), then a shift to the centre of
  gravity and eigenvalues (`nalgebra::SymmetricEigen`). Units: mm, g/cm³ → g
  (1 mm³ · 1 g/cm³ = 0.001 g).
- The exact kernel (`ExactSolid::measure_full`) serves as a cross-check in the tests.
- `src/gui/material.rs`: the window.
