# 2.7 Rib / Web

## 1. Purpose

Rib makes a thin supporting wall from an **open** sketch profile: a rib in the corner of a bracket, a grid
of ribs in the bottom of a box. The rib is filled with material up to the walls of the part, so the
final shape comes from a few lines, without drawing a closed contour.

Two kinds:
- **Rib** (Parallel to Sketch Plane): the material spreads in the sketch plane towards the
  body; the thickness is along the sketch normal. Example: a triangular rib in the corner of an "L".
- **Web** (Normal to Sketch Plane): the profile is extruded along the sketch normal (down to the
  bottom); the thickness is in the sketch plane, on both sides of the line. Example: a grid of
  partitions in a box. The profile may be open or closed.

## 2. Inputs

- **Profile:** curves of one sketch (lines, arcs, circles, splines); several chains make a
  network, intersecting or not.
- **Solids:** the body the rib is added to.
- **Centers** (Boss): sketch points on the profile.

## 4. Browser and editing behaviour

- In the browser it is `Rib1` (tooltip "Rib of Sketch2") with the consumed sketch under it. Edit Feature
  opens the same panel; Properties shows the direction, thickness, depth and bosses.

## 5. Edge cases and errors

- The profile does not face a body (To Next finds no face): "the web does not reach a face (use
  Finite)" / "the rib profile does not face the body (use Finite)".
- A closed profile with Parallel to Sketch Plane: "a rib parallel to the sketch plane needs an
  open profile".
- Draft with Hold At Root where the top thickness becomes ≤ 0: "Draft: the web gets no
  thickness at the top".
- Zero thickness or depth: "… must be greater than 0".
- Boss diameter ≤ the thickness: "Boss: the diameter must be larger than the thickness".
- A refusal leaves the part unchanged (test `rib_refusals_leave_the_part_unchanged`).

## 6. Implementation

- **Data:** `part::rib::RibFeature` (`Feature::Rib`, format revision 14), `RibDirection`,
  `RibSide`, `RibExtent`, `DraftHold`, `RibBoss`; `PartDocument::add_rib`; `rib::chains` joins
  the curves into chains by their shared ends; `rib::default_curves` — the initial selection.
- **Where the body is:** `Rays` — rays against the view triangles (`entry` gives entering and
  leaving the material; an even/odd number of crossings tells whether a point is inside).
- **Web:** a strip of the thickness around the chain (offset with mitres; a closed chain — a ring),
  extruded along the normal (`exact::extrude`, with a slope for Taper/Draft); the bosses are circles at
  the points, extruded from Offset to the same depth.
- **Rib:** a polygon from the curve and its "shadow" on the body (the hit points, slightly
  inwards, simplified with Douglas–Peucker), extruded ± along the normal.
- All tools are joined with the body (`exact::combine`, Join); records a `ToolSet`, so
  Pattern/Mirror copy the rib.
- **Names:** `<id>:Side:Left` / `Right` / `End0` / `End1` (Web), `<id>:Side:Top` / `Root` (Rib),
  `<id>:Side:Outer` / `Inner` (closed Web), top `<id>:Start`, bottom `<id>:End`, bosses
  `<id>:Boss<k>:Side:Wall`.
- The faceted kernel has no Rib: "Rib needs the exact geometry kernel".
- **GUI:** `gui/coil_rib.rs` (`RibDraft`, selectors Profile → Boss Centers, Extend Profile only
  when the ends do not reach the body, preview, OK/Apply/Cancel, Edit Feature, Properties),
  prompts EN/BG, F1 → this file.
- **Tests:** `tests/coil_rib.rs` — Web To Next in an open box (56·2·18), the three thickness
  sides, Extend Profile on/off, Finite, a network of 3, Taper and Draft (Top/Root), bosses,
  Rib in the corner of a bracket (triangle 200 mm² × 4), refusals, save/load, editing, Rib in a
  hollowed box (Shell), Rib → Fillet at the root, rectangular pattern of ribs; `tests/perf.rs`.
