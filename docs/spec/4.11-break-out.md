# 4.11 Break Out (drawing stage D-S6)

> A local section of a view: inside a closed sketch profile drawn on the view, the material between the viewer
> and a depth is cut away, so the inside of the part shows there, hatched where the cut passes through
> material. The rest of the view stays as it was.

## 1. Purpose

A hole, a pocket or a mechanism inside a part often needs only a small look inside, not a full section view.
**Break Out** removes material inside a profile the user sketches on the view, down to a depth, and draws what
lies behind: the cut face (hatched), the edges and surfaces behind it. The view keeps its place, scale and
everything outside the profile.

## 2. Inputs

1. Place Views › Modify › **Break Out** (alias BO), or the right-click menu of a view.
2. The view (the selected view when the command starts). It needs a sketch with a closed profile: without one,
   a view sketch opens (the drawing's sketch tools) and the dialog opens on Finish Sketch.
3. **Boundary Profile**: the closed profile of a sketch of the view — taken by itself when there is only one
   unconsumed sketch, else picked.
4. **Depth**: its type and what it needs (below).
5. **Display**: Show Hidden Edges, Section All Parts.
6. OK.

### What is drawn

| Behaviour | Inventor (measured) | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Inside the profile, in front of the cut | left out: model edges there disappear (an 80 × 50 × 12 plate: a Ø10 blind hole inside the profile no longer drawn at depths 1, 5 and 10 mm) | ✅ curves cut where they cross the profile or the plane; the occluding faces in front cut by the profile's triangles; by test and visually |
| The profile itself | drawn as visible lines (the rectangle drawn on the view appears among the view's curves) | ✅ |
| The cut face | hatched with the section hatch (the Object Defaults' Section Hatch) | ✅ the section of the faces by the plane, clipped by the profile (a 20 × 20 profile round a Ø10 hole: 400 − 25π mm² hatched); its outline inside the profile drawn; visually checked |
| Behind the cut, inside the profile | the edges and faces behind the cut show, hidden ones by the view's style | ✅ edges behind the cut face are hidden by it; through openings (the hole) they show |
| Outside the profile | unchanged | ✅ |

## 5. Edge cases and errors

- No closed profile: `Break Out: the sketch has no closed profile`.
- A depth in front of the whole part (nothing cut): the view is unchanged, the profile still drawn.
- A depth behind the whole part: everything inside the profile is cut away (an empty hole in the view).
- Several loops in one profile: each is a break out of its own with the same depth.

## 6. Implementation

- `drawing::BreakOut` in `DrawingView::break_outs` (skipped when empty; old builds draw the view whole, so no
  format revision): the profile (closed loops in the view plane, true coordinates), the depth type and its
  data (From Point: the picked point as a model point and the value; To Hole: From Point at depth 0 with
  `hole` = the hole's key, the point from `drawing::break_out_hole_point`; To Sketch: `BreakOutDepth::ToSketch`
  = the sketch's view, the line in its plane and the sketch, resolved by `drawing::break_out_sketch_plane` into a
  depth and a slope — `hlr::OutCut::depth_at` is affine in the view plane), `suppressed`.
- `hlr`: a break out is a removed region of the view: the profile's prism along the view direction, cut at the
  depth plane (square to the view). Triangles in front of the plane are clipped out of the occluders inside the
  profile (the profile split into convex pieces), curves in front of it inside the profile left out, the cut
  face hatched (the section of the faces by the depth plane, clipped by the profile), the profile drawn. Boxes (the profile's, each segment's, each cut piece's) skip the work away from it: the
  2 500-hole plate with a round profile 1.1 s (6.4 s before; `tests/perf_drawing.rs`, budget 3 s).
- GUI `gui/drawing_breakout.rs`: the command (`DrwCmd::BreakOut`: the view, its sketch `SketchFor::BreakOut`, the
  dialog over the sheet's right top, the From Point pick in another view), Edit Break Out, Edit Break Out Sketch (`SketchFor::BreakOutEdit`), Delete Break Out — the one under
  the pointer (`BreakOut::under`).
