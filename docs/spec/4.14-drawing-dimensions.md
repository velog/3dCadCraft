# 4.14 Drawing dimensions: every type of the Dimension command (drawing stage D-S3)

> The general Dimension command (**D**) takes what is picked and makes the fitting type: a length, a
> distance, a diameter, an angle. This spec lists every type and pick order Inventor's command offers and
> what the Dimension Type menu switches between. Edit Dimension (text, tolerance, fits) is spec 4.7.

## 1. Purpose

A drawing's dimensions must be able to say what a part's maker needs: the angle between three points, the
length along an arc, the distance to where two edges would meet, a shaft's diameter on its side view. All
of them stay tied to the model's edges by persistent names and update with the view.

## 2. Inputs

Geometry of **one** view, picked in order; then a click for where the text goes. Points are end points,
circle centres, midpoints (a dot on hover), a circle's four quadrant points (dots on hover) and implied
intersections. Curves are straight edges, circles and arcs.

## 3. Options

Status: part 1 of stage D-S3 is the types; part 2 the Dimension Type menu of a placed dimension, per-end
arrowheads and extension lines; part 3 multi-vertex leaders and hole note templates.

## 4. Browser and editing behaviour

The dimensions are annotations of the sheet (not in the browser), edited by double-click, moved by their
text. Each new type works with Edit Dimension, layers and the PDF like the others.

## 5. Edge cases and errors

- An edge that is gone leaves the dimension orphaned (the usual red `?`).
- Parallel lines for Intersection: "Dimension: the lines are parallel"; the pick stays.
- A tangency keeps its side (near or far from the other point), so it stays when the part changes.
- A click on empty paper with only a circle picked places its diameter or radius; a click on other geometry
  makes a distance. To place the text over an edge, move it afterwards.

## 6. Implementation

- `annot::PointRole`: `Mid`, `Quadrant(u8)`, `Tangent(bool)` (near the other point), `Intersection`
  (with `PointRef::other`, the second edge); `DimKind`: `AngleThree` (`Dimension::c`), `ArcLength`,
  `ArcAngle`, `LinearDiameter`. New variants: format revision 69.
- Chord Length is an `Aligned` dimension between the arc's own two ends.
- `annot::point_in` gives a role's point; `arc_sweep` the arc's real direction from its drawn piece.
- GUI: `DimDraft` (`third`, `kind`, `cross`), `geometry_under_with` (the extra dots only in Dimension).
