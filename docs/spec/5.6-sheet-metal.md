# 5.6 Sheet Metal

Status: ✅ done · 🟡 partial · ⬜ to do · ⛔ blocked by another feature.

> **Research document first** (the owner's rule for this module, 2026-10-24): this file gathers how Inventor
> 2027 behaves, what SolidCraft (MIT OR Apache-2.0) already implements that we can adapt, and a staged plan.
> Every row starts ⬜. Rows marked *(to confirm)* were written from general Inventor knowledge and must be
> checked against the 2027 help page before that row is implemented.

## 1. Purpose

Parts made from sheet stock of one thickness: plates, flanges and bends that a press brake can make, and
the **flat pattern** (the part unfolded) that a laser / punch cuts. Every bend is developed from the sheet
metal **rule** (thickness, bend radius, K-factor or bend table, reliefs), so the flat pattern is right for
the shop. Exports the flat pattern to DXF for the cutter and feeds drawings (bend lines, bend table) and
the BOM (flat size, `Sheet Metal Length/Width/Area` properties).

## 2. Inputs

- A **sheet metal part** (Inventor: a part document of subtype *Sheet Metal*; a normal part can be
  converted). Sketches as in parts; edges and faces of the sheet body.
- The active **Sheet Metal Rule** (a style) and the per-feature overrides.

## 4. Browser and editing behaviour

- Sheet metal features are history features like the part's (Edit, Suppress, reorder, EOP).
- The Flat Pattern is a node after the folded model; editing a folded feature updates it.
- Rule and Defaults changes regenerate every feature that uses the rule's parameters.

## 5. Edge cases and errors

- A flange or fold that would intersect the sheet itself: an error naming the feature (`Flange: …`).
- Bend radius 0 or K outside 0..1: refused by the rule editor.
- A body that is not of constant thickness cannot be converted (the face found is reported).
- A closed shape without a rip cannot be unfolded: the flat pattern reports it.

## 6. Implementation (research)

### 6.1 What SolidCraft has (`crates/doc/src/sheet.rs`, 6 800 lines; `sheet_convert.rs`; `crates/engine/src/cmd/sheet.rs`; `crates/io/src/dxf.rs`)

- **Model:** a sheet body is kept **as its flat pattern**: the base panel, flanges hanging off panel edges
  (each a bend zone of width BA = θ·(R + K·T) followed by a flat panel), cut-outs, all in the flat frame;
  folding maps flat coordinates through the parents' bends. The folded solid is built from extruded
  pieces (panels; bend zones as annular sectors) joined together; the flat solid is the outline extruded.
- **Rules** (`SheetRule`): thickness, K-factor, bend radius, relief width / depth, corner relief and its
  shape (notch, round, rectangular, tear), hem gap, gap — as **expressions** (`Thickness` usable).
- **Features:** `SheetBase` (Face), `SheetContour` (Contour Flange), `SheetFlange` (several edges, height,
  angle, radius, position inside/outside/middle, flip, per-edge values), `SheetHem` (flat hem), `SheetFold`
  (sketch line, fold location, fixed side), `SheetUnfold` (unfold all / refold), `SheetConvert` (from a
  plate or a shelled body with chosen bend edges).
- **Flat pattern:** `flat_solid`, `outline`, `bends()` (bend lines), `flat_size`, DXF writer.
- Bounded work budgets everywhere (no runaway geometry).

### 6.1a S1 as built (2026-10-25)

- `kernel::exact::sheet_metal` wraps `solidcraft_doc::sheet` (Face, flanges, flat solid / size / bends,
  edge names); `part::sheet_metal`: the rule, `Feature::SheetFace` / `SheetFlange` (format revision 19),
  `PartDocument::sheet_models` (runtime, one model per sheet body, kept in the build cache), the step key
  includes the rule. Faces of the sheet are named by the sheet (`Face1:<panel>:top`, bends
  `Face1:Bend:<k>`).
- A Hole or Cut on the sheet is not in the sheet model yet (S2): a Flange after one is refused with the
  reason instead of dropping it.
- GUI `gui/sheet_metal.rs`. Perf: Flange on 4 edges + flat pattern 18 ms.
- Revision 21 (2026-10-26): `Feature::SheetOp` (Hem, Fold, Contour Flange, Unfold, Refold, Cut — one
  feature type, `kind` in the JSON); the Cut is our `cut_through` in `vendor/solidcraft-doc` (regions
  mapped to the flat through the panel's placement, kept as cut-outs). Not in SolidCraft, still ⬜: Bend,
  Corner Seam, Rip, Punch Tool, Contour Roll, Lofted Flange. Perf: Hem + 20-hole Cut + Fold + Unfold +
  Refold + DXF 443 ms.
- Revision 20 (2026-10-25, after the owner asked for SolidWorks / Solid Edge): Width Extents
  (`FlangeWidth`, our `add_flanges_spans` in `vendor/solidcraft-doc`: the flange ends as if a cut-out ended
  its edge, so the base keeps its edge beyond and gets a bend relief), corner relief shapes, and
  `Feature::SheetConvert` (edge names of a converted sheet: base outline `base:E<k>`, panels
  `<id>.<k>`). Convert of a box to a tray: 17 ms.
