# 3.9 Representations and variants

Status: ✅ done · 🟡 partial · ⬜ to do · ⛔ blocked by another feature.

## 1. Purpose

One assembly, several ways of looking at it. Inventor keeps three independent lists in the browser's
folders *Representations › View*, *Representations › Position* and *Model States* (ours: *Variants*):

- **View representation:** what is seen (visibility, appearance, camera);
- **Positional representation:** where the mechanism stands (overrides of relationship values, grounding,
  placement), e.g. *open* and *closed*;
- **Variant:** what it consists of (suppressed components and relationships, so also its BOM).

**Variants** is our name for what Inventor calls Model States (owner's choice, 2026-11-03: a plain word, not to be
confused with Part Families); rows below compare with Inventor's Model States. Changing one does not change the others. A drawing view picks one of each.

## 2. Inputs

The assembly's occurrences and relationships. Nothing is written into a part (rule 8).

### 3.3b What a variant holds

Inventor gives each member its own parameters, iProperties and BOM. Ours goes further for an assembly, at the owner's wish
(2026-11-03): patterns too, and which variant of a part each occurrence places.

| Item | Assembly | Part | Our status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Suppression | components, relationships | features | ✅ |
| Values | relationship values (a free joint is held); a position's override still goes over them | every parameter | 🟡 parameters checked in the app; relationship values by tests with the solver |
| Component patterns | count and spacing or angle; copies are made and removed when the patterns are placed | — | ✅ 2026-11-03 (activated: 4 → 8 copies; a cell: 8 → 6) |
| Properties | Part Number, Stock Number, Description, BOM Structure | the same | 🟡 a part variant's Part Number checked in the BOM; the rest by tests |
| Material | — | by name, from the library | ✅ 2026-11-03 (the BOM and the view follow it) |
| BOM of a component | Counted or Reference per occurrence | — | 🟡 tests; not driven in the app yet |
| The part's variant per occurrence | `DocRef::variant`: Properties and the menu of the occurrence, or a section of the assembly's table | — | ✅ 2026-11-03 (two variants of one part side by side; Properties row; BOM rows apart); the menu entry and the table's section not driven yet |

A part in a chosen variant is built once and kept while the part and that variant stay the same (`LibCache::variant_part`), so
the view, the solver, the BOM and drawings read it the usual way, through `with_part`. A face that a variant does not have
makes the relationships on it sick. A subassembly's chosen variant is ⬜ (`tree` walks the saved state).

Known limits: a cell of the BOM window edits the document's Properties, not a chosen variant's (⬜ for rows of a variant); a pattern
copy suppressed by hand comes back when a count changes it away and back; appearances, the settings of a feature that are not
parameters, a sketch's placement and other Properties are not part of a variant.

## 4. Browser and editing

Folders above the relationships: *Representations* (*View*, *Position*) and *Model States*. Rows have a
check mark when active, a lock when locked; the right-click menus carry the commands of §3. Keys and
ribbon: `docs/UI.md`.

## 5. Edge cases and errors

- A positional representation that does not solve: nothing changes, the status bar says
  `Position: <name> does not solve` (rule 5).
- A deleted occurrence or relationship leaves every representation.
- Representations refer to occurrences and relationships by their ids, which are never reused.

## 6. How we implemented it

`assembly::reps` (GUI-free): `Representations` in `AssemblyDocument::reps`. The occurrences and relationships
always hold the state of the active representations, so every reader (view, picking, solver, BOM) works
unchanged. `sync` writes changes of that live state into the active representations; `activate_*` first syncs,
then composes the new state. A drawing gets its copy through `Representations::composed`.

The occurrence and relationship maps are lists of pairs (`id_map`): a document is read through the internally
tagged `DocumentData`, where the text keys of a JSON map cannot become `u32`. Old builds ignore `AssemblyDocument::reps`
and `DrawingView::reps`, so there is no format revision.

Variants of a part are `part::variants` (`PartDocument::variants`): the part holds the active variant's state the same way.
`part::variants::Table` is what the window shows for both kinds of document, and every correction is a list of `Set`s.
