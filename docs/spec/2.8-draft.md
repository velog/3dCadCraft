# 2.8 Face Draft (draft for mould release)

## 1. Purpose

Face Draft tilts selected faces by an angle relative to a pull direction, so that
the part can come out of the mould in casting, injection moulding or pressing. The command is
also used simply to tilt a face. The draft is defined by the faces, the direction, the angle and a
fixed reference: an edge of each face, a fixed plane or a parting
line. With a plane or a parting line the faces can be tilted on one or on both
sides of the reference.

## 2. Inputs

| Type | Selectors (in this order) | Angles |
|---|---|---|
| **Fixed Edge** | Pull Direction → Faces | Draft Angle |
| **Fixed Plane** | Fixed Plane → Faces | Draft Angle (+ Draft Angle 2 with Asymmetric) |
| **Parting Line** | Pull Direction → Parting Tool → Faces (→ Static Edges with Move Parting Line) | Draft Angle (+ Draft Angle 2) |

- **Pull Direction:** a planar face, a work or origin plane (its normal), a straight edge,
  a work or origin axis (their direction). Flip reverses it. With Fixed Plane the direction is
  the normal of the plane (with Flip).
- **Fixed Plane:** a planar face, a work or origin plane.
- **Parting Tool:** a plane (planar face, work, origin) or a 2D sketch with lines.
- **Faces:** the faces to tilt. With Fixed Edge the place of the click selects the fixed edge.
- **Static Edges:** the edges about which the face rotates with Move Parting Line.

## 4. Browser and editing behaviour

- In the browser it is `Face Draft1` (id `FaceDraft1`). The tilted faces keep their names, so
  sketches and features on them stay connected. A split face (Symmetric, Asymmetric,
  Parting Line) has two parts with the same name; later selection uses the point (hint).
- The parting sketch is a child of the feature (as Extrude consumes its profile).
- Edit Feature opens the dialog with the selection. The part is shown as it was before the draft.

## 5. Edge cases and errors

All refusals have the form `Face Draft: <reason>`; the part stays as it was, and on
regeneration the feature becomes "sick". It never falls back to the faceted kernel.

- An angle ≥ 90° in absolute value: "the draft angle must be between −90° and 90°", for
  the second — "the draft angle 2 …".
- A fixed edge that is not perpendicular to the direction: "the fixed edge of … is not
  square to the pull direction (not supported yet)".
- A parting plane that is not perpendicular to the direction: "the parting plane is not
  square to the pull direction (not supported yet)".
- A sketch with an inclined line or lines at different heights: "the parting line is not at one
  height along the pull direction (not supported yet)"; a sketch without lines — "the parting
  sketch … has no lines".
- Move Parting Line without edges on one side: "select the static edges above/below the
  parting line"; edges at different heights — "not supported yet".
- A plane that splits the body into more than two pieces: "not supported yet".
- A face whose neighbours cannot be extended, or a round that does not take part:
  the kernel's reason ("the geometry kernel cannot build it (…)").
- Known problems in SolidCraft ([SOLIDCRAFT_ISSUES.md](../SOLIDCRAFT_ISSUES.md)):
  a face with inclined neighbours (#1126), the end face of a flange (#390).

## 6. Implementation

- Kernel (`kernel::exact`):
  - `draft(solid, faces, neutral, pull, angle)` through `sk::draft`: convex body →
    half-spaces; otherwise planar and cylindrical faces (the cylinder becomes a cone);
  - `draft_two_sided(solid, faces, plane, pull, above, below)`: `sk::split_by_plane`,
    draft of each half with its own neutral plane and angle (the upper one along the direction,
    the lower one against it), then `sk::boolean` Union. The parts are found by the history
    (`FaceOrigin::Face`), the names stay.
- Part: `part::draft::DraftFeature` (`Feature::Draft`; format revision 4, and Parting Line,
  Symmetric/Asymmetric and Move Parting Line — revision 9), `DraftType`, `DraftExtent`,
  `PartingTool`, `PartingSide`, `PullRef`; `faces_with_blends` (Automatic Blending),
  `two_sided`, `select_boundary`, `fixed_edge`; `part::exact::draft`; `PartDocument::add_draft`.
- GUI: `gui/draft.rs` (`DraftDraft`, `DraftPick`, `draw_draft_overlay`), the selectors in
  `command::flow::draft_selectors`, the rows and the menu in `gui/command_ui.rs`, the dialog in
  `gui/panels.rs` (`draft_dialog`).
- Tests:
  - kernel: `kernel::exact::tests::draft_*` (box, one face ±, cylinder, L-prism,
    two-sided: 5 height/angle combinations, one side, Move Parting Line, errors);
  - part: `tests/draft_cases.rs` (Symmetric/Asymmetric ×6, cylinder, L-prism, Parting
    Line with a plane and 5 directions, with a sketch, Move Parting Line Top/Bottom/Both, Automatic
    Blending with One Way and Symmetric, refusals, editing and saving, Shell and Fillet before/after);
    `tests/matrix.rs` (the "draft …" cases); `tests/compat/r9`;
  - the selectors: `command::flow::tests`;
  - speed: `tests/perf.rs` ("face draft 3° symmetric …").
