# 2.4 Fillet (rounding edges)

Status: ✅ done · 🟡 partial · ⬜ to do · ⛔ blocked by another feature.

## 1. Purpose

Rounds the edges of the body. On a convex edge it removes material (**round**), on a concave edge it adds material (**fillet**). The command belongs to the placed features: it needs no sketch and takes edges from the model.

## 2. Inputs

- edges: one by one, a loop (Loop), all edges of a feature (Feature) or of a body (Solids), All Fillets / All Rounds;
- radius (expression, tolerance);
- several **sets** of edges with different radii in one feature.

## 4. Edge cases and errors

- The radius is larger than an adjacent face → `Fillet: the radius is too large for the edge`.
- The edge disappeared during an edit → it drops out of the set; if none is left → sick feature.

## 5. Implementation (stage 1)

- `part/blend.rs`: `BlendFeature { edges: Vec<EdgeRef>, kind: Fillet { radius } | Chamfer {...} }`.
- `EdgeRef` = the names of the two adjacent faces + a hint point (the middle of the edge at selection time). This way the edge is found even after Booleans, when the edges themselves have no names (rule 2).
- **Exact kernel** (`kernel::exact`, `part/exact.rs`): the edges are passed to the SolidCraft kernel as points on them. The new faces get names after the replaced edge, e.g. `Fillet1:Round[Box1:Front|Box1:Right]`, and the corner pieces `Fillet1:Corner:<n>`. Old names from the faceted kernel (`Fillet1#4:Side:Face`) are found by feature and point (`blend::face_matcher`). Radius too large: the kernel refuses, the part is built faceted and the feature gets an error.
- **Straight edge between two planar faces**: in a plane perpendicular to the edge, the profile between the two faces and the arc of the round is built. It is extruded along the length of the edge and subtracted from the body (convex edge) or added to it (concave edge).
- **Circular edge between a planar face and a cylinder** (exact or faceted): the same profile in a plane through the axis is revolved by 360°.
- A corner where rounded edges meet: what remains is the intersection of the two bodies, not a sphere as in Inventor (🟡).
- All edges of the feature are computed on the body **before** it. Otherwise the second edge is shortened by the first and a step is left in the corner (seen in the visual test).
- A chamfer or round that reaches an already rounded corner stops at the tangent line (a small step; Inventor smooths the corner) (🟡).
