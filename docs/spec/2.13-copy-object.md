# 2.13 Copy Object

## 1. Purpose

Copies faces or whole bodies of a part into a new surface body, a composite, one solid or several solids —
for example to keep a face as reference surface before it is changed, to make a solid from a closed set of
faces, or (with Delete Original) to move geometry out of a body. Inventor: 3D Model › Modify › Copy Object.

## 2. Inputs

- **Objects**: faces (Select = Face) or bodies (Select = Body) — solids or surface bodies, picked in the view or
  in the browser. A pre-selected face or body fills the selector (rule 17). In Body mode a face pick takes its
  body.
- Faces are `FaceRef`s (persistent name + a point), bodies persistent names (rule 2).

| Create New **Group** | a group in the construction environment | needs the construction environment | ⛔ blocked by the construction environment (not planned yet); shown disabled |

## 4. Browser and editing behaviour

- The feature is `CopyObject<N>` (format revision 40, `BodyOp::CopyObject`) with the Copy Object icon; its tooltip
  says what was copied into what.
- New bodies are named after the feature: `CopyObject1`, further ones `CopyObject1#2`, `#3` … (surface bodies under
  Surface Bodies, solids under Solid Bodies). Each copied face is `<body>:<source face name>`
  (`CopyObject1:Extrusion1:Top`), so faces of a copy never share a name with their source.
- Edit Feature reopens the panel with the feature's options; selectors left empty keep the feature's picks.
- Deleting the feature removes the copies (and brings back what Delete Original removed).

## 5. Edge cases and errors

- Nothing selected: *select the faces or bodies to copy*.
- A face or body that no longer exists after an upstream edit: the feature's health.
- Solid / Multiple Solids from faces that do not close: *the faces do not close a volume* (Inventor refuses too).
- Group, Select Existing: not offered (disabled).
- Every refusal leaves the part unchanged (rule 5).

## 6. Implementation

- Data: `part::copy_object::{CopyObjectOp, CopySelect, CopyOutput}`.
- Geometry: `part::copy_object::copy_object` — faces through `kernel::exact::face_sheet` (SolidCraft's
  `copy_faces`), contiguous sets found by shared edges, several sheets sewn by `surface_ops::stitch`; bodies through
  `exact::transformed` (identity); Delete Original through `exact::delete_faces`.
- GUI: the planned panel of `gui/part_planned.rs` with logic (`gui/surface_cmd.rs::copy_object_from`).
- Tests: `tests/copy_object.rs`, `tests/compat/r40`.
