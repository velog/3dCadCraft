# 5.8 Plastic part features — Grill, Boss, Rest, Snap Fit, Rule Fillet, Lip

Statuses: ✅ done and checked in the app, 🟡 partial (what is missing; built and tested by volumes but not driven in the app, where
it says so), ⬜ to do.

## 1. Purpose

Six commands of the *Plastic Part* panel that add the usual details of a thin-walled moulded part in one
feature each. All six are `BodyOp` variants (format revision 64), built with tools and Booleans on the exact
kernel. Each works on a target body (Solid selector; empty = the only / nearest one).

## 2. Inputs

| Command | Inputs |
|---|---|
| Grill | closed boundary profile; optional island profiles; rib and spar curves; all on planes parallel to the boundary |
| Boss | sketch points (From Sketch) or points + a direction (On Point) |
| Rest | closed profile on a plane between the wall's faces |
| Snap Fit | sketch points (From Sketch) or points + two directions (On Point) |
| Rule Fillet | rows: source (features or faces), rule, radius |
| Lip | path edges on a flat rim; guide face or pull direction |

A sketch pick carries the clicked point: a region, a curve or a point of that sketch is chosen by it, and only
that lights up under the cursor and once picked. A sketch picked as a whole (its browser row) gives every region
/ every Center Point. Lines of a Grill's sketch that end in the air do not divide its regions, picked as ribs or not.

## 3. Options

**The panel's picture** (ours, not Inventor's static help images): Grill, Boss, Rest, Snap Fit and Lip show the
section of what they build, to scale, from the panel's current values, under the tabs. What exists has the panel's control
colour, what the command makes the scheme's highlight colour (as its preview in the view; the picks have the blue), the path a dot in its selector's colour. The sizes of the tab on show are dimensions; values with no line of
their own (an angle, a size that is zero now) stand in a row under the section. A click on a dimension's text opens the
same expression field as the panel's row (rule 13); Enter applies it and picture and preview follow. A tab the options
chosen do not use says so. Heights the command finds from the model (a boss's run to the wall) are drawn at a typical
size. Selectors the options have no use for are hidden and take no pick, and every pick is drawn in its selector's
colour.

Head geometry, from the mating plane towards the wall (our reading of the names; Help gives only the list):
a plate *Shank Height* thick with the shank hole, a raised seat ring (*Clamp Diameter*, *Clamp Height*), the
head recess (*Head Diameter*) through the wall; outside diameter = head + 2 × wall.

## 4. Browser and editing

One browser node per feature, icon of its command; Edit Feature reopens the panel with the values. Numeric
fields are parameters where listed in `BodyOpFeature::fields`.

## 5. Edge cases and errors

- Non-positive sizes: refused before any geometry (`validate`).
- A centre with no body in its direction, a profile leaving the body: feature error, no change (rule 5).
- Kernel failure of a Boolean or fillet: feature health, never a faceted rebuild.

- Known kernel limits: Rib Radius and Blend Radius are often refused (the error names the row); two bosses with ribs exactly in a
  row along the sketch's X, 20 mm apart, were refused in a test (the wall's triangles overlap round the ribs' feet); a Fillet with ribs
  rounds the post's foot only, and takes ~6 s per boss.
