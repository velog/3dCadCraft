# 5.2 Loft (transition between sections)

## 1. Purpose

Loft makes a smooth body through several sections: a square → circle transition for a duct, a housing,
a blade, a bottle, a ring. The shape between the sections can be guided by rails (Rails), by a
centre line (Center Line) or by given areas along the length (Area Loft); the ends can
have conditions (Conditions), and the correspondence between the points of the sections can use manual pairs
(Transition). An end can be a point (an apex).

## 2. Inputs

- **Sections:** in the order of selection — closed profiles from sketches, loops of planar faces,
  a point (only first or last). Two consecutive sections cannot lie in one plane.
- **Rails:** one or more curves (from 2D sketches or edges) that touch **every** section
  and guide the shape between them.
- **Center Line:** one curve; the intermediate sections stay perpendicular to it. It does not need
  to pass through the sections.
- **Area Loft:** a centre line + placed sections (Placed Sections) with an area or a scale.
- **Map Point:** points on the outlines of the sections (one per section), in the order of the sections.
- **Solids:** which body the feature works on (with > 1 body).

## 4. Browser and editing behaviour

- In the browser it is `Loft1`, with the consumed sketches of the sections, the rails and the centre line under it.
- Editing any of these sketches rebuilds the transition (the cache key includes all of them).
- Edit Feature rolls the model back to before the feature (rollback).
- A refusal (a rail does not touch a section, a wrong point etc.) leaves the part unchanged; on
  regeneration — the feature's "health", never an invalid body.

## 5. Edge cases and errors

- Fewer than two sections: "Loft: select at least two sections".
- A point not at an end: "Loft: a point can only be the first or the last section".
- Two consecutive sections in one plane: "Loft: sections 1 and 2 lie in the same plane".
- A rail does not reach a section / passes beside it: "rail 1 does not reach section 2", "rail 1 does
  not touch section 2: it crosses the section's plane 7.50 mm from its outline"; a rail that
  goes back: "does not run through the sections in their order"; rails that cross each other:
  "the rails cross each other between two sections".
- Center Line / Area Loft without a curve: "select the centre line"; a line that does not run in the order
  of the sections — refusal.
- A placed section outside the transition: "a placed section at 1.500 lies outside the loft".
- A condition on a middle section, Tangent for a sketch, Direction for a face, Smooth, a rounded apex,
  conditions with rails or a centre line, conditions with Closed Loop — clear refusals.
- Map Point: an incomplete set ("point set 1 needs one point on each of the 2 sections"), a point
  far from the outline ("map point 1 is … mm off section 2"), a different order along the sections.
- Self-intersection: every station must be entirely in front of the previous one's plane (in the direction of
  the transition); otherwise refusal "the loft would cross itself: its surface folds back … mm behind a
  section …" (e.g. sections larger than the bend radius of the centre line). The kernel
  returns such a body without an error, so the check is ours (`check_folds`).
- A ring-shaped section without guides: a hollow body (never solid — SolidCraft #1184).
- Shell of a transition with B-spline faces: the kernel refuses the offset — a clear refusal, the body stays.

## 6. How we implement it

- Data: `part::loft::LoftFeature` (`Feature::Loft`, format revision 12; the new options
  use fields already saved — no new revision is needed), `LoftSection`, `LoftCondition`,
  `LoftGuide`, `PlacedSection`, `MapPoint`.
- Without guides and conditions: `kernel::exact::loft` (sections on their own planes; the holes —
  a separate loft and Cut) and `loft_to_point`.
- With guides, conditions, Map Point, Closed Loop: `kernel::exact::loft_guided`
  (`src/kernel/exact/loft.rs`):
  1. every section becomes a closed loop of degree-7 Bézier pieces (line, cubic and
     spline pieces exactly; arcs to ~2·10⁻⁷ of the radius) in a frame carried from section to
     section with the smallest rotation between the normals (along a centre line — RMF);
  2. **matching**: the contacts with the rails and the Map Point points become shared vertices, the parts
     between them are split to the same count; without them — by directions from the centre;
  3. **stations**: cubic Hermite of the origins and of the control points (Catmull-Rom with a tangent
     length as for an arc — the ring stays round), the conditions set the end
     derivatives; then the station is fitted to the rails / the centre line / the area;
  4. the kernel (`sk::loft_matched` / `sk::loft_matched_ring`, our additions in `vendor/`,
     keep the order of the segments) makes one smooth B-spline face per segment
     through all stations (≤ 50).
- Face names: `Loft1:Start`, `Loft1:End`, `Loft1:Side:<curve of the first section>`
  (the ring has no Start/End).
- GUI: `gui/loft.rs` (tabs, selectors, Area Loft, conditions, Map Point), `gui/sweep.rs`
  (selection in the view, preview), `command::flow::loft_selectors`.
