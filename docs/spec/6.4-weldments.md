## 1. Purpose

A **weldment** is an assembly whose parts are welded into one inseparable item. Manufacturing goes in
three steps and the model keeps them apart, so a drawing can show each one:

1. **Preparation** — material removed before welding (bevels, groove chamfers, cope holes), acting on
   the assembled parts without changing their part files;
2. **Welds** — the weld beads: solid fillet and groove beads (volume and mass, interference) or
   cosmetic beads (a symbol and a length, light), each with its welding symbol;
3. **Machining** — material removed after welding (facing, bores through several plates, holes),
   cutting through parts *and* beads.

The BOM shows the weldment as one item (Inseparable); the drawing shows any of the states with weld
symbols, caterpillars and end fills taken from the model.

## 2. Inputs

- An assembly (new from a weldment template, or converted).
- Its components (parts, frame members from 6.1); faces and edges of them for the beads.
- The weld standard (ANSI, ISO, DIN, BSI, GB, GOST, JIS) and the bead material.

## 4. Browser and editing behaviour

- Under the assembly: **Preparations**, **Welds**, **Machining** folders after the components; each
  lists its features (with their participants), the Welds folder its beads and symbols (beads under the
  symbol that consumed them).
- Double-click a group = edit it; while one is active, later groups are rolled back (Edit Feature
  behaviour of parts, applied per group).
- Beads reference faces / edges by `(occurrence path, persistent name)`: a component that moves takes
  its beads with it; a face that disappears makes the bead sick (health), never silently dropped.
- Suppressing a component suppresses beads that need it (health shows why).

## 5. Edge cases and errors

- Fillet weld between parallel faces: `Fillet Weld: the face sets do not meet at an angle.`
- From-To planes not parallel: `Fillet Weld: the extent planes are not parallel.`
- A bead larger than the faces it sits on: the bead is clipped to the faces (Inventor) *(to confirm)*.
- A groove weld with no gap between the sets: `Groove Weld: there is no groove to fill.`
- Convert to Weldment of an assembly with assembly features: they go to the group chosen in Feature
  Conversion; the conversion is one Undo step and asks for confirmation (one-way).
- A weldment inside a weldment: the sub-weldment is one item; its beads stay its own.

## 6. Implementation (proposal)

- **Data**: `AssemblyDocument::weldment: Option<Weldment>` (format revision; `None` = a plain assembly):
  `standard`, `bead_material`, `groups: [WeldGroup; 3]` — Preparation and Machining hold assembly
  features (⛔ 3.10 §3.8 assembly features: participants, per-occurrence cut bodies), Welds holds
  `WeldBead { kind: Fillet | Groove | Cosmetic, sets, extents, size, contour, intermittent, symbol }` and
  `WeldSymbol` (sides, fields per standard, consumed bead ids). Ids never reused.
- **Beads as geometry** (`assembly::weld`): a fillet bead is a sweep of its section (legs, contour
  arc) along the contact curve between the two face sets (the intersection curve of the faces, or the
  shared edge), trimmed by the extents; intermittent = segments of it. A groove bead = the volume
  between the sets closed by the faces' outer edges (a Loft / Boundary Patch between their edges, 5.2 /
  5.4). Built in **assembly coordinates** from the occurrences' bodies, one solid per bead, named
  `<bead id>:Face`, `:End1`, `:End2`. Cosmetic beads are only polylines + an area.
- **Regeneration**: parts (unchanged files) → Preparation cuts per occurrence (cached per part
  signature + placement + features) → beads → Machining cuts on the occurrences' bodies and the beads.
  Display, picking, Interference, Physical Properties and drawing views read the result of the active
  state.
- **Drawings**: a view stores `WeldmentState`; welding symbols and caterpillars are resolved from the
  model's beads (like hole tables from `PartDocument::holes`).
- **GUI**: `gui/weld.rs` — the Weld tab (Preparation, Welds, Machining toggles; Fillet, Groove,
  Cosmetic, Welding Symbol, Bead Report, Weld Calculator), panels on the `command_ui` flow (face set
  selectors with Continue), preview of the bead on the worker thread. Right-click menus (rule 17): two
  faces → Fillet Weld; an edge → Cosmetic Weld; a bead → Edit, Create Welding Symbol, Delete.
- **Tests**: fillet bead volume = section area × length (equal legs a: a²/2 · L) for a T joint of two
  plates; Pappus for a bead round a tube; mass with the bead material; the states in drawings; save /
  load; compat fixture; Inventor as the reference for bead volume and mass of a few joints.
