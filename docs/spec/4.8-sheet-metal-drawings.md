# 4.8 Sheet metal in drawings: flat pattern views, bend lines, bend notes

Status: ✅ done · 🟡 partial · ⬜ to do · ⛔ blocked by another feature.

## 1. Purpose

Draw a sheet metal part's flat pattern on a sheet, the way the shop needs it: the developed outline with
its cut-outs, every bend's centre line and extents, and a note on each bend (direction, angle, radius).
The flat comes from the part's sheet metal model (`docs/spec/5.6-sheet-metal.md`), so a changed flange,
thickness or rule updates the view and its notes.

## 2. Inputs

- A sheet metal part (`PartDocument::sheet_models` not empty): its flat pattern (`flat_patterns`) and bend
  lines (`flat_bend_lines`).
- The drawing's styles: dimension style (precision, decimal marker) for the notes, the Bend Centerline and
  Bend Extent layers.

### 3.2 What the flat pattern view draws

| Item | Inventor behaviour | Restrictions | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Outline and cut-outs | the flat's visible edges (Visible layer) | — | 🟡 |
| Bend centre lines | one per bend, on layer **Bend Centerline** (chain line, 0.25 mm) | — | 🟡 |
| Bend extents | the two lines where each bend zone ends (allowance / 2 each side), on layer **Bend Extent** (continuous, 0.5 mm) | — | 🟡 |
| Layers | both layers are in the Style Editor (on / off, colour, line type, weight); drawings made before them get the defaults | — | 🟡 |
| Punch centres, Center of Gravity, Bend Up / Down colours | Inventor options of the flat view | — | ⬜ |

### 3.3 Bend Notes (BN)

| Option | Inventor behaviour | Restrictions | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pick | click a bend centre line of a flat pattern view; the command repeats; Esc ends | one note per bend (a second click is refused); no window selection yet | 🟡 |
| Text | direction, angle and radius: `UP 90,00° R2,00` — the drawing's dimension style decides precision, trailing zeros and the decimal marker | the format cannot be edited yet (Inventor's Bend Note style) | 🟡 |
| Placement | along the bend line, centred on it, above the bend zone, readable from the bottom or the right | — | 🟡 |
| Move | drag the text; it keeps its offset when the bend moves | — | 🟡 |
| Associativity | the note belongs to the nearest bend (within a tenth of the view); a removed bend orphans it (marked, never deleted) | — | 🟡 |

## 4. Browser and editing behaviour

- Edit View switches a base view between Folded Model and Flat Pattern; the view's notes stay with their
  bends.
- Bend notes are deleted with their view, as every annotation.

## 5. Edge cases and errors

- Flat Pattern on a part that is not sheet metal: the option is disabled.
- A part whose flat pattern cannot be built: the view shows the folded part.
- Bend Notes with no flat pattern view on the sheet: `Bend Notes: no flat pattern view on this sheet ...`.

## 6. Implementation

- `drawing::SheetView` on `DrawingView::sheet_metal` (base views), `DrawingDocument::flat_of`,
  `LinesJob::Model::flat`.
- `drawing::hlr::flat_model` / `flat_lines`: the flat solid's model moved into the flat's own frame
  (`transformed`), bends projected into `ViewLines::bends` (`ViewBend`).
- `drawing::render::view_extras` draws the bend lines (`DrawingStyles::bend_layers`, `ObjectDefaults::
  bend_center_layer` / `bend_extent_layer`).
- `annot::AnnotationKind::BendNote { view, bend, offset }`, `nearest_bend`, `bend_note_text`.
- GUI: Sheet Metal View in the Drawing View dialog (`gui/drawing.rs`), the flat on the drawing worker
  (`gui/drawing_bom.rs` `ModelJob::lines`), Bend Notes in `gui/drawing_sheet.rs`.
- Format revision 26; fixture `tests/compat/r26`. Tests: `drawing::tests::flat_pattern_view_and_bend_note`,
  `compat::drawing_r26_reads`.
