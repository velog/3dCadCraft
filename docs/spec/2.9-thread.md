# 2.9 Thread

## 1. Purpose

Thread puts a thread on a cylindrical face: an external one on a shaft or an internal one in a
hole. In Inventor the thread is always *cosmetic*: it is stored as data (standard, size, class,
length) and shown in the view as a texture. The geometry does not change. We also have a
**modeled** thread (as in FreeCAD): a real helical groove, useful for 3D printing. There is also
a **print clearance**: it widens an internal thread and narrows an external one by a given value.

## 2. Inputs

- **Face:** a cylindrical face. A click near one of its ends sets where the thread starts.
- **Specification:** type (table), size, designation (pitch), class, Right/Left Hand.
- **Depth:** the full length, or Offset + Length.
- **Modeled thread** and **Thread clearance** (ours).

### 3.1 Display of the cosmetic thread

In Inventor the cosmetic thread is drawn as a *texture* (a picture of a thread) on the
cylindrical face itself, not as separate geometry: this keeps the model light. The texture
covers only the length of the thread and follows the face under rotation and lighting in the
shaded and realistic styles, inside a hole and outside a shaft. The Textures On/Off command in
View → Appearance does not affect threads (they stay visible); they are hidden with *Display
thread in model* on the feature itself. Ours: a procedural texture in the shader, no image.

## 4. Browser and editing behaviour

- In the browser it is `Thread1` (hint: the designation, cosmetic or modeled). Edit Feature
  opens the same panel; Properties shows the face, the thread, the length and the expression
  fields.
- The modeled groove is the face `Thread1:Thread`. A cosmetic thread does not change the body.

## 5. Edge cases and errors

- "The thread does not generate geometry" (the owner, 2026-10-19): the modeled groove was
  skipped without a message when the middle of its zone was outside the body. Fixed, see
  2.3 §5; tests along the GUI path (`gui::hole::tests::modeled_thread_*`) check that `part.body`
  and the view's scene have the groove faces.

- The face is not cylindrical: "Thread: select a cylindrical face".
- The diameter does not match the size (shaft: major diameter, hole: minor diameter or the
  drill) by more than 15 % of the size: "an M12 thread does not fit a Ø8 shaft" (SolidCraft
  #2705/#2969: diameter tolerance).
- Offset + Length longer than the face: "the thread is longer than the face".
- A modeled thread over 200 turns: a message.
- The thread starts at a free end (the groove runs out into the air by one pitch); with a
  partial length it ends abruptly at its end.

## 6. Implementation

- `part::hole::ThreadFeature` (`Feature::Thread`, format revision 6), `ThreadSpec`
  (`internal_groove`, `external_groove`, `note`), `CosmeticThread`; `PartDocument::add_thread`,
  `PartDocument::cosmetic_threads`.
- `part::exact::thread`; the modeled groove — `kernel::exact::cut_threads` after all features.
  The smooth `kernel::exact::helix_tool` (`sk::sweep_helix`) is available, but the exact
  Boolean with it is too slow (see 2.3 §5).
- GUI: `gui/hole.rs` (`ThreadDraft`, `thread_lines` — preview only), `gui/hole_panel.rs`.
- Texture: `part::hole::ThreadBands::of(&part.cosmetic_threads)` (sleeve by `BAND_SAG`, offset
  by `BAND_LIFT`, a phase at every vertex), `gui::renderer::SceneGeometry::add_thread_bands`,
  pipeline `threads` (blending Dst·Src + Src·Dst, no depth write, after the opaque faces and
  before the edges), shader `vs_thread` / `fs_thread` in `gui/viewport.wgsl`. The mesh is built
  in `build_scene` (when the model changes), never per frame. Tests: `thread_bands_*`
  (`part::hole`), `thread_texture_on_hole_and_shaft`, `pattern_copies_carry_the_thread_texture`
  (`tests/holes.rs`), `viewport_shader_validates`; perf: "thread texture: mesh of 100 tapped M8
  holes".
- Tests: `thread_on_a_shaft_cosmetic_and_modeled` (cosmetic, wrong size, modeled 10 mm),
  `cut_threads_facets_only_the_zone` (right- and left-handed), fixture `r6/hole_r6`.
