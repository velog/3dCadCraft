# 2.14 Thicken/Offset

Status: ✅ done · 🟡 partial · ⬜ to do · ⛔ blocked by another feature.

## 1. Purpose

Adds or removes material on chosen faces of a solid by a thickness (a slab grown from the faces and
joined to, cut from or intersected with the solid, or kept as a new solid), turns a surface body into a
solid of that thickness (*Quilt*), or copies faces moved along their normals into a surface body
(*Output: Surface*, Inventor's offset surface). Inventor: 3D Model › Modify › Thicken/Offset.

## 2. Inputs

- **Faces** (Select: Face): faces of one body — a solid or a surface body. A picked face brings the faces
  tangent to it when Automatic Face Chain is on.
- **Quilts** (Select: Quilt): whole surface bodies.
- **Solids** (multi-body parts): the solid the result is combined with. Without it: the solid holding the
  faces, or the only solid.

## 4. Browser and editing behaviour

- The feature is `Thicken<N>` (one id prefix for thickness and offset), under the body it changes; a New
  Solid or an offset surface is a body of its own (`Thicken<N>`).
- Edit Feature (double-click, menu) reopens the panel with the feature rolled back; the faces are kept by
  name and a point.
- Right-click on faces offers Thicken/Offset with the faces filled in (rule 17).

## 5. Edge cases and errors

- No face / quilt, distance ≤ 0, Surface with Quilt or Symmetric: refused before any geometry work.
- Faces of two bodies: refused (*the faces belong to different bodies*).
- A face that no longer exists after an edit upstream: the feature's health (*the face … no longer exists*).
- Thicken of every face of a closed solid (Join outward / Cut inward): the faces are offset into the new
  solid (`exact::offset_solid`); a Boolean of the solid with its own shell finds no intersection.
- A Boolean that fails on a slab touching the body face to face is retried with the slab overlapping it.
- A surface with a point or an edge that is sharp in places and smooth in others (a cone tip of an egg
  profile drawn with free ends, a loft from a circle through a rectangle): refused with that reason.
- Inward across a sharp edge of a surface (a loft of rectangles): refused by the kernel (*inside
  corner*), the other side works.
- Cut / Intersect that leaves nothing: health. Kernel failures (a thickness that folds a face over, a
  face kind the kernel cannot move): health, never a faceted rebuild.

## 6. Implementation

- Data: `part::body_op` — `Feature::BodyOp(BodyOpFeature { op: BodyOp::Thicken(ThickenOp) })`, format
  revision 31. Later body operations (Delete Face, Move Bodies, Copy Object, Replace Face, the surface
  commands) are further `BodyOp` variants.
- Geometry: `kernel::exact::thicken` — `face_sheet` (SolidCraft `copy_faces`), `thicken_sheet`
  (`thicken`), `offset_sheet` (`offset_surface`); `part/exact.rs::body_op` combines with the solid.
- Names: a face's own copy keeps its name, the moved copy is `<id>:Offset:<face>`, a wall
  `<id>:Wall:<edge>`; a joined solid keeps its body name.
- GUI: `gui/thicken.rs` (panel, picks, preview, Edit Feature, Properties).
- Tests on hard surfaces (`tests/thicken_surfaces.rs`): sphere shells exact; a whole spline egg out,
  in and symmetric against Steiner's formula; half / quarter eggs = ½ / ¼ of the whole; revolved eggs
  of 90 … 300° = their share of the whole; offset surfaces; smooth surface lofts (the out / in average
  = area × t); a tube swept along a spline (Pappus).
