# 2.17 Emboss / Engrave

Status: ✅ done · 🟡 partial · ⬜ to do · ⛔ blocked by another feature.

## 1. Purpose

Raises or recesses a closed sketch profile (later also sketch text) on a part: lettering, logos, pads
and shallow pockets that follow the face they sit on. Inventor: 3D Model › Create › Emboss.

## 2. Inputs

- **Profile**: a sketch with closed regions, clicked in the view or the browser. Its regions become the
  profiles (each its own tool). The sketch is consumed by the feature, as an Extrude's.
- **Wrap to Face**: one face to roll the profile onto.

## 3. Options

| Option | Inventor's behaviour | Restrictions | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Profile | sketch geometry or text; single regions can be picked | | 🟡 a whole sketch; single regions and text ⬜ ⛔ sketch text (1.11) |
| Emboss from Face | raises the profile above the face by Depth; the top is the face offset, the walls run along the sketch's normal | the whole profile must lie over the body, or the command is refused | ✅ flat and curved faces |
| Engrave from Face | recesses it by Depth into the face | same | ✅ |
| Emboss/Engrave from Plane | no depth: from a sketch outside the body the profile grows to the body; from a sketch inside the body it cuts away everything on that side | a direction pointing away from the body is refused | ✅ |
| Depth | the offset of the two face types | above zero; not used by From Plane (disabled) | ✅ |
| Direction | face types: the sketch's normal or against it; the face reached first that way is the one worked on (from inside the body, the one the ray leaves through); a sketch lying on the face works on it both ways. From Plane also has Symmetric: each side on its own | Symmetric only for From Plane | ✅ |
| Taper | From Plane only: positive widens the tool away from the sketch (draft for moulds), negative narrows it | below 89° | ✅ |
| Top Face Appearance | an appearance for the raised top only, not the walls | | ⬜ button disabled |
| Wrap to Face | the face types: rolls the profile onto one face; a length along the sketch is the same arc length on the face, the walls are radial, the top is the face offset. It stops at a face square to the wrapped one | one face, planar or conical; no spline faces | 🟡 rectangles along the axis and round a cylinder; curved outlines and cones ⬜ |

## 4. Browser and editing

- A row **Emboss1** with the sketch nested below it; double-click or Edit Feature opens the panel with
  the feature's values; a selector left empty keeps the feature's picks. ✅
- Depth and Taper are the feature's parameters. 🟡 stored, equations in the panel's fields ⬜.

## 5. Edge cases and errors

- A profile running past the face's edge: `Emboss: the profile must lie entirely over a face of the body`.
- A direction that misses the body: refused, the part unchanged.
- **Deviation:** From Plane with the sketch lying on the body's face changes nothing in Inventor and
  gives no message. Here it is refused with the reason, since a feature without an effect only confuses.
- A plane tangent to a cylinder, direction away from it: Inventor embosses the far side of the cylinder;
  here the point on the skin is the face worked on (the near side).

## 6. Implementation

- `part/emboss.rs`: `EmbossOp` (`BodyOp::Emboss`, format revision 62).
- A flat face square to the sketch: a prism from the face (`exact::extrude`). A curved face: the face's
  offset thickened (`offset_sheet` + `thicken_sheet`), intersected with the profile's prism. Tools stand
  a hair into the air (engrave) or the body (emboss), so no tool face lies on the body's.
- Wrap: `kernel::exact::emboss::ring_sector`. The kernel's own `emboss_cylinder` was not used: it takes
  only a body made of one complete cylinder and two caps.
- Faces: `<id>:<k>:…` with `k` the profile from 1, so `<id>:<k>:End` is a top or a floor.
- GUI: the planned panel with logic (`gui/surface_cmd.rs`); `tests/emboss.rs`; `examples/emboss_demo.rs`.
